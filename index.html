    How to Play Scoundrel

    You start with 20 health. Survive the dungeon by resolving cards in each room.

    • Room: 4 visible cards. Either Avoid once (not twice in a row) or resolve 3 cards.
    • Carry: After resolving 3 cards, the 4th card becomes the first card of the next room.
    • Weapons (♦2–10): Equip to reduce monster damage. Equipping a new weapon discards the old one and its stack.
    • Potions (♥2–10): Heal up to 20. Only one potion per room has effect.
    • Monsters (♣/♠): Bare-handed damage is full value. With a weapon, damage is monster − weapon (minimum 0).
    • Weapon sequence rule: A weapon can only be used on monsters whose value is less than or equal to the last monster it fully defeated. Example: if your last defeated monster with that weapon was 8, you may use it on 8, 7, 6... but not 9.
    • Win: Clear the dungeon deck. Score is remaining health.
    • Lose: Health reaches 0. Score is negative sum of monster values left in deck.
